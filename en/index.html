The Story · 02

A new chapter for Salalah

Salalah Eye rises above Al Hafa Beach as the Khareef season begins. At 52 metres above the Arabian Sea, thirty enclosed cabins offer the full panorama of Dhofar's mountains, coastline, and the ancient port of Al Hafa in a single 22-minute rotation.

91% of Khareef visitors speak Arabic · 1,048,000 visitors to Dhofar in Khareef 2024

The Khareef · 08

July in Salalah is like nowhere else on Earth

The Arabian Khareef — a monsoon season unique to Dhofar — transforms Salalah's mountains from desert to lush green between June and September. Temperature drops to 17–22°C. Mist rolls in from the sea each morning. Salalah Eye opens on the first day of Khareef — 15 July 2026.
